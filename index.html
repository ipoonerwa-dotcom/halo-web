How to mint

Six steps, one BNB quote

The sale is a kind-3 flywheel on GangFlywheelPad. Parameters are fixed at create. The pad cannot pause the sale, change those parameters, or take the BNB it holds.

  1. 1

    Hold a .ai Agent ID

    Minting checks the Agent ID gate. Testnet uses 0x24D0…5B2a. A mainnet sale will use 0x60B9…022F. Full addresses are in the Agent ID section below. A wallet without an Agent ID cannot mint.

  2. 2

    Pay BNB, not a token transfer

    The quote asset is WBNB. Before the pool opens, a share is 0.002 BNB. Add 1% for the platform fee bucket. Wrapping and the mint call happen together. A plain BNB transfer to the pad, or to any token address, does not mint.

  3. 3

    Stay inside the Agent ID quota

    Each Agent ID starts with 10 shares, and that quota covers both the pre-open sale and the minting after open. One transaction can take 1 to 10 shares. An invite adds one share to the referrer when the invited wallet mints for the first time, and only if the referrer has already minted and holds an Agent ID. Inviting yourself does not count. There is no social-task claim.

  4. 4

    Before the pool opens

    The wallet receives 100,000 HALO per share. The pool is reserved the same number. Tokens cannot be transferred yet. Selling back pays the mint price minus a 1% exit fee. After seven days without progress that exit fee is 0. The 1% platform fee is not returned.

  5. 5

    Share 10,000 opens the pair

    The mint that sells share 10,000 launches in that same transaction. With a zero creator cut of the principal, the pair is seeded with 20 BNB and 1,000,000,000 tokens. LP is minted to the dead address. The creator can open earlier once 3,000 shares are sold. Early open seeds only what has actually been raised: at 3,000 shares that is 6 BNB and 300,000,000 tokens.

  6. 6

    Keep minting after the open

    Post-open minting is on. The whole batch uses the last share’s tier. Token amounts then follow the pool, and new liquidity is locked to the dead address. The rules are in the next sections.

Post-open mint

The pool opens. The mint does not stop.

Kind 3 is built so the sale that fills share 10,000 is the same sale people keep minting. Net BNB from those later mints is added to the official pair, and the new LP goes to the dead address.

One price for the batch

If a mint crosses a tier, every share in that transaction is charged at the last share’s tier. The price is not the sum of each share.

Tokens follow the pool

After the open, the token amount is set from the pool price, using the spot price, the block-start price, the recent high, and the 30-minute TWAP as floors. This page does not invent that number.

The price levels off

There are 353 tiers. Share 4,440,000 is 8.07× the opening price, 0.01614 BNB. Every later share stays at 0.01614 BNB. Minting does not end there. Supply is capped at 1,000,000,000,000 tokens.

SharedDividendVault

Minting records a holder baseline in the vault. A sell-back writes it again. Claims are on the vault. Eligibility uses kind 3’s 60% hold line. Buy tax and sell tax are each 1%. Half of that 1% is recorded for holders, a quarter goes to the platform fee bucket, and a quarter to the creator.

When a mint is refused

A post-open mint is refused while the pool is more than 10% under its average or its recent high, or when this wallet’s last 30 minutes would move more than 2% of the pool’s quote.

What the sale cannot do

The creator’s cut of the principal is zero. Only the creator can open early, and only from 3,000 shares. The sale has no pause and no per-sale renounce. Open-early does not expire. The 1% mint fee is separate from tax and goes to the platform fee bucket.
